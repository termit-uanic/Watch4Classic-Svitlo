## Title (max 30)
Wattlo: Power Outage Schedule

## Short description (max 80)
Power outage schedule on phone and Wear OS watch: countdown, widgets, alerts

## Full description (max 4000)
Is the power on right now, how long until it changes, and what comes next? Wattlo answers at a glance: on your phone, on the home screen and on your Wear OS watch.

WATCH (Wear OS)
• Tile: the state, a countdown, the time of the next change and a ring or strip of the coming hours
• Watch face complication: "ON 4:53" / "OFF 0:37" with a progress arc
• The app itself: the outages for today and tomorrow
• Works on its own, without the phone; the countdown is calculated by the watch

PHONE (Android)
• Now: the state and the countdown, the day ring (one ring of 24 hours or two of 12) and the list of outages
• Queues: your main queue plus extra ones with names of your own ("parents", "sister") to see several at once
• Widgets in three sizes: the state, the state with a ring, today and tomorrow. Each widget has its own queue, background (dark, tinted by the state, transparent) and ring settings
• Reminders a few minutes before the power goes off and comes back, quiet hours, notifications when the schedule changes
• How the tile and the watch app look can be set on the phone; the shared settings are synchronised between the phone and the watch

REGIONS
21 regions: 20 oblasts (Poltava, Kharkiv, Dnipropetrovsk, Odesa, Kyiv, Lviv and more; not yet Donetsk, Luhansk, Kherson or Chernivtsi) and the city of Kyiv. Pick your region and queue in a short setup wizard.

SIMPLE AND PRIVATE
No accounts, no ads, no analytics. The app downloads public schedules by itself and keeps everything on your devices. Languages: Ukrainian, Russian, English. Open source (MIT).

IMPORTANT
Wattlo is an unofficial app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or representing Ukrenergo, the Ministry of Energy, any regional power company, DTEK, YASNO or any other grid operator. The schedules come from public sources (poe.pl.ua, the official schedule of the Poltava power company, and bezsvitla.com.ua) and may differ from the actual outages. Please also follow the official announcements of your power company.
